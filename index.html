Thought Hunter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026
Developer: Easeful Labs
Contact: easefullabs@gmail.com

Thought Hunter ("the app") is a CBT-inspired psychoeducation game for adults (18+). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. In short: what you write and play stays on your phone. We have no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

1. Not medical advice

Thought Hunter is a psychoeducation game that helps you notice thinking patterns. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition and is not a substitute for professional care or therapy. If you feel unsafe or are in crisis, please contact your local emergency number (112 in Türkiye) or a qualified professional right away.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and is not sent to us or to any server we operate:

  • Your settings (app language, dictation language, reminder time, sound preference);
  • Game progress (caught thoughts, points, streaks, play time, days played, selected life area);
  • Your emotion choices, diary and reflection entries, and any thoughts you type or dictate;
  • Counters used to decide when to ask you to rate the app.

Matching a thought you typed to a thinking pattern happens on your device. You can erase everything at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

3. Voice dictation (microphone)

If you tap the microphone to dictate a thought, the app asks for the microphone permission and uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (provided by your device and Google). Audio may be processed by that service under its own privacy policy. The app itself does not record, receive or store your audio.

4. Crash and problem reports

To fix bugs, the release version of the app uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google). When the app crashes or when you use "Report a problem", it may send: crash details (technical error trace), app version, device model, Android version, language, the problem category you picked, and an automatically generated app-installation identifier. These reports contain none of your diary entries, thoughts or typed text. Google's processing is described in the Firebase privacy information. Analytics collection is switched off.

5. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. This uses the notification permission and a boot permission so reminders continue after a restart. No data is sent for this.

6. Internet access and other services

  • The internet permission is used by crash reporting and when you open a link, share the app, or are asked to rate it (Google Play in-app review).
  • When you use the share function, the text you choose to share goes to the app you pick; we do not receive it.

7. What we do not do

We do not show ads, do not create user accounts, do not sell or rent personal information, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

8. Children

The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. If you believe a minor is using it, please contact us.

9. Your rights

Because your game content stays on your device, you control it directly. For crash-report data, you may ask us at the e-mail above about access or deletion, and, depending on where you live (for example under the Turkish KVKK or the EU GDPR), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, inform you in the app or in the store listing.
